02

A mark of connection.

One recognizable signature, with a layout for every space. Use the supplied artwork and preserve its proportions.

Core logo family

Give it room.

Start with clear space equal to ¼ of the visible symbol height on every side of the complete lockup. Verify against the selected master.

Match the surface.

Use light or dark artwork for its intended background. Choose a mono file for true single-color production.

Keep it legible.

Switch to compact or symbol-only when the descriptor becomes too small. Never stretch, rotate, retype or add effects.

Purpose in every color.

Navy gives structure. Blue invites action. Green brings emphasis. Cloud leaves room to breathe. Select a swatch to copy its HEX value.

Core HEX values come from the printed labels on the identity board. RGB artwork; approve print conversions with your supplier.

AaNavy on Hub Green
6.50:1 / normal text
Design for readability. White on Hub Green is 2.54:1 and fails the normal-text threshold. Use navy text, or use the supporting Deep Green #047857 with white text. Test controls and states in context. WCAG contrast guidance.
