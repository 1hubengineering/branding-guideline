Return to brand guidelines · Documentation and references

Export conventions. PNG and SVG logo files are transparent. White artwork can look blank on a white preview; use a dark surface. JPG files include white or navy backgrounds. Raster size names indicate the longest artboard edge, including clear space. Icons and avatars are in 04-icons/. Auto-theme SVGs and a manifest template are in 05-web-kit/.

All logo lettering is outlined. No fonts, external images, or linked resources are required by the fixed-theme SVG masters.
