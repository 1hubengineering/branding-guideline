# Reconstruction notes

Reference: AndHub Engineering Brand Identity Board.png, supplied by the user.

## Preserved
- Angular, three-part symbol with a split blue left face, central ribbon and green right face.
- Crossbar-free custom A and the uppercase ANDHUB wordmark structure.
- Name: ANDHUB / ENGINEERING PLC.
- Tagline: IDEAS. ENGINEERED.
- Stacked and horizontal arrangements represented in the source board.
- Light, dark and monochrome treatments.

## Reconstructed or newly standardized
- Symbol outline coordinates and curves were manually reconstructed from the large primary example.
- Wordmark contours were rebuilt as clean geometric paths, not extracted from an original font.
- ENGINEERING PLC and IDEAS. ENGINEERED. were re-created with outlined Inter Display, fitted and tracked to visually follow the reference. This is not a claim to have identified the source lettering's font.
- Gradients were visually approximated. Minor source textures, shadows and image noise were not reproduced.
- The dark symbol uses a pale-to-teal center ribbon following the source's dark presentation.
- Flat-palette, compact, wordmark-only, reverse-white and deployment-specific variants extend the supplied presentation.
- Spacing, divider treatment and artboards are internally consistent proposed standards, not recovered original construction measurements.

## Quality checks
The build checks SVG syntax, missing external dependencies, outlined text, raster dimensions/alpha, each ICO's embedded sizes and internal HTML references. Representative large, small, light, dark and monochrome variants are also visually inspected. Web media-query and app-icon compatibility still need verification in the actual deployment environment.

RGB artwork only; no CMYK or spot-color conversion is represented. No font files are distributed.
